# Support

## Ask a question

- **The website.** [herdling.zog-tech.com/support](https://herdling.zog-tech.com/support) has
  answers to common questions. The [guide](https://herdling.zog-tech.com/docs) explains setup,
  pairing, every setting, voice and troubleshooting.
- **GitHub Discussions.** Ask how to use Herdling, share an idea or show your setup.
  Open the **Discussions** tab of this repository.
- **Email.** [admin@zog-tech.com](mailto:admin@zog-tech.com) for anything that does not fit in public.

## Report a bug

Open an issue with the **Bug report** form. Please tell us:

- the platform (iPhone or Android) and its version
- the Herdling version and build number (the App Store, Google Play or TestFlight page shows it)
- the `herdlingd` version or the date you installed it, and the computer's operating system (macOS or Linux)
- the herdr version
- the agent kind (Claude Code, Codex, Pi, OpenCode or another agent)
- the steps to repeat the problem, and what you expected

Do not post passwords, private keys, push tokens, pairing codes, host names, IP addresses or
screenshots of real sessions. Before you post a log, remove these items.

## Ask for a feature

Open an issue with the **Feature request** form. Say what you want to do, not only what to build.

## Security problems

Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Problems with herdr or an agent

Herdling shows what herdr and the agents do. It cannot fix a problem in them. For help with
herdr, see [herdr.dev](https://herdr.dev). For an agent, see the documentation of that agent.
Herdling is not made by or affiliated with the makers of herdr, Claude Code, Codex, Pi or OpenCode.

## Response time

This is a small project. We read every issue, but we cannot promise a reply time.
